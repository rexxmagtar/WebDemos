ASMR Puzzle Lab

Two kept prototypes: a tilt-maze and a bento pin-and-sand sorter, tuned for feel and order-of-operations play.

Physics maze + bead tray

1. Tilt Tray Pearls

Roll every pearl into the soft goal pads. Pearls are solid: they can’t pass through one another or share a cell.

Twist: every tilt moves all active pearls. Lose: one pearl drops in a drain.
Pull-the-pin + kinetic sand

2. Pin Sand Bento

Pull seven staggered, angled baffles in the right order so each stream lands in the matching cup.

Twist: deeper wells and diagonals change routes. Lose: wrong color in a cup.
